What it does

A small workspace for finding good prices, kept simple.

Ranks by real savings

Sorts by percent off and rupees saved together, so a ₹300 saving outranks a ₹3 one.

Tracks price drops

Remembers each scan and labels products that are new or cheaper than last time.

Scans in parallel

Several requests at once, with retries and automatic back-off. Stop whenever you like.

Filters that matter

Search, category, brand, maximum price, minimum discount, stock and saved items.

A shopping list

Quantities, total and total savings. Copy it, or open every product at once.

Export and share

Download a CSV, or copy the top deals as text for a chat message.

Light and dark

Grid or list, works on phones, with keyboard shortcuts on desktop.

Private by design

Uses your own session. No servers, no analytics, no accounts.

Questions

Is it safe? Does it see my data?

It runs inside your browser tab using your own Flipkart Minutes session. There is no server and no analytics. Saved items and price history stay in your browser's local storage.

Why do I get no results at first?

Flipkart Minutes shows products only for a delivery location. Open flipkart.com, pick your Minutes address or pincode, then scan again.

Is it official?

No. It is an independent tool, not affiliated with Flipkart Minutes, and it only reads pages your account can already see.

Will it keep working?

Flipkart Minutes can change its internal APIs without notice. The parser reads responses generically to tolerate small changes, and the tool tells you when a scan returns nothing.

How does price-drop tracking work?

After each scan it remembers every price in your browser. On the next scan, new products are labelled New and cheaper ones Price drop.

Can I try it without Flipkart Minutes?

Yes. Use Try the demo to explore the full interface with sample data.
