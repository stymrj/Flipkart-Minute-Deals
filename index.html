Telegram alerts

Get deals on your phone.

Hunter can message you on Telegram the moment a deal crosses your threshold. Each deal is sent once, and again only if it gets cheaper.

  1. Create a bot

    In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot and follow the prompts. Copy the bot token it gives you.

  2. Say hello to it

    Open your new bot and press Start, so it is allowed to message you.

  3. Find your chat ID

    Message @userinfobot, or open api.telegram.org/bot<token>/getUpdates and copy the number after "chat":{"id":.

  4. Connect Hunter

    In Hunter's sidebar, open Telegram alerts, paste the token and chat ID, press Send test message, then switch alerts on.

  5. Optional: let it scan by itself

    Set Auto-scan in the sidebar (for example every 15 minutes) and keep the tab open. New deals reach Telegram without you checking.

Use the userscript for alerts. Some shops block bookmarklets from talking to Telegram; the userscript is allowed to. Your token is stored only in this browser.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it safe? Does it see my data?

It runs inside your browser tab using your own Flipkart Minutes session. There is no server and no analytics. Saved items and price history stay in your browser.

Why do I get no results at first?

Flipkart Minutes shows products only for a delivery location. Open flipkart.com, pick your Minutes address or pincode, then scan again.

Is it official?

No. It is an independent tool, not affiliated with Flipkart Minutes. It only reads what your account can already see.

Will it keep working?

Flipkart Minutes can change its internal APIs at any time. The parser reads responses loosely to survive small changes, and the tool tells you when a scan returns nothing.

How does price-drop tracking work?

After each scan the tool remembers every price in your browser. Next time, new products are labelled New and cheaper ones Price drop.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Press Try the demo at the top to explore the full interface with sample data.
